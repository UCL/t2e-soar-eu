\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Three measurable axes classify European streets into eight types of urban fabric
  \item First pedestrian-scale morphological atlas of 626 urban centres in 28 countries
  \item Intense--Attached fabric shows the shortest service walks, even at matched density
  \item Green-space access inverts the gradient; the trade-off weakens across cities
  \item Open dataset and adjustable thresholds support benchmarking and monitoring
\end{itemize}
