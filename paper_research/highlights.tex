\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Three measurable axes classify 12.3 million European streets into eight types of urban fabric
  \item First pedestrian-scale morphological atlas spanning 626 urban centres in 28 countries, from open data
  \item Intense--Attached fabric shows the shortest walks to services; the gap persists at matched density
  \item Green-space access inverts the commercial gradient, but the trade-off weakens across cities
  \item Open dataset and adjustable thresholds support continental benchmarking and monitoring
\end{itemize}
